# Analysis: OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra

## Main claim

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra on September 3, 2026 and says it is the first broadly deployed OpenAI model to reach the `Critical` cybersecurity capability threshold under its Preparedness Framework.

## Assessment

**SUPPORTED · 87/100 · HIGH**

The assessment is scoped to the launch and to how OpenAI classifies the model under its own framework. It is not an independent certification of Astra's real-world cybersecurity capabilities.

## Evidence basis

### OpenAI

OpenAI's official material directly states the release date and the `Critical` classification under its Preparedness Framework. It is primary evidence for the company's action and its own classification.

### Reuters

Reuters independently confirms the Astra launch and reports the context of growing scrutiny around agent safety. This supplies a separate corroborating line for the event, but it does not turn OpenAI's internal classification into an independent industry standard.

## Counter-evidence

The bounded review found no material counter-evidence against the claim that OpenAI announced the model on September 3 and classified it this way. Absence of counter-evidence does not independently establish every future property or behavior of the model.

## Uncertainty and scope

- `Critical` remains a label within OpenAI's classification system.
- This analysis does not interpret that label as a probability of harm.
- This analysis does not assess the long-term effectiveness of safeguards after broad deployment.
- The assessment may require revision if new material evidence appears.
