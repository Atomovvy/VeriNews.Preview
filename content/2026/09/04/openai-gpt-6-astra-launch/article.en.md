# OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra with its highest cyber capability threshold yet

## In brief

OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra on September 3. The company classifies it as its first broadly deployed model to reach the `Critical` cybersecurity capability threshold under its own Preparedness Framework. Reuters independently confirms the launch and reports growing scrutiny around agent safety.

## Why this assessment

The launch and the scope of the official classification are directly supported by OpenAI. Reuters independently confirms the launch and the safety context. No material counter-evidence was found against this narrowly framed claim in the reviewed material.

## What is confirmed

- OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra on September 3, 2026.
- OpenAI describes Astra as its first broadly deployed model at the `Critical` cybersecurity level under its own Preparedness Framework.
- Reuters independently confirms the model launch.

## Important qualification

`Critical` is a category in OpenAI's own Preparedness Framework. VeriNews does not treat that label as an independent universal AI-safety standard or as a probability of harm.

## What remains unknown

This material does not assess the long-term effectiveness of Astra's new safeguards after broad deployment.
