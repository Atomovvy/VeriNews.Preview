In brief

OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra on September 3. The company classifies it as its first broadly deployed model to reach the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold; Reuters independently confirms the launch and reports growing scrutiny of agent safety.

Important qualification

Critical is a category in OpenAI's own Preparedness Framework. VeriNews does not treat that label as an independent universal AI-safety standard or as a probability of harm.
