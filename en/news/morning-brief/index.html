1. Bus disruptions expected during the 56th Bielsko Family Bicycle Rally

On September 22, Bielsko-Biała Public Transport published a notice about service disruptions planned for Sunday, September 27 in connection with the 56th Bielsko Family Bicycle Rally. Temporary changes to bus operations are expected from 9:30–11:00 and later along subsequent sections of the route. The notice directly matters to residents planning Sunday trips by public transport.

2. Two bus stops will receive new names from September 28

Bielsko-Biała Public Transport announced on September 22 that the names of two stops in the city will change from Monday, September 28. This is practical passenger information because the new names will appear in timetables and passenger-information systems.

3. Car-Free Day: free city buses for eligible drivers

On September 22, Bielsko-Biała is taking part in European Car-Free Day. People who meet the city's promotion conditions can use all municipal bus lines free of charge that day after presenting the required vehicle and identity documents. Locally, the initiative is intended to encourage drivers to try public transport instead of their cars.

4. The 56th Bielsko Family Bicycle Rally is scheduled for September 27

Local media reported on September 22 that registration remains open for the 56th Bielsko Family Bicycle Rally on Sunday, September 27. The route is approximately 32 km long. The event matters both to participants and other residents because it is a major recreational event accompanied by temporary traffic and public-transport changes.
