1. DeepSeek is set to brief the UN Security Council on AI risks

Reuters reported on September 22 that representatives of DeepSeek are expected to take part this week in a UN Security Council briefing on artificial intelligence and international security. Reuters said representatives of other AI companies are also expected to participate. The key significance is that frontier-model risk is increasingly being discussed not only by labs and domestic regulators, but also in an international-security forum.

2. NVIDIA launches DSX Ready for AI-factory infrastructure

NVIDIA launched DSX Ready, a qualification program for infrastructure components that meet applicable requirements of its DSX AI-factory reference design. The first categories cover battery energy storage systems and coolant distribution units. It is a practical signal that power and cooling constraints are becoming as central to scaling AI infrastructure as accelerators themselves.

3. The U.S. and China discuss an AI incident-notification mechanism

The Associated Press reports that U.S. and Chinese officials have discussed a mechanism for notifying each other about AI incidents that could affect national security ahead of a leaders' meeting. This does not amount to broad agreement on AI policy, but it points to a narrow potential area of cooperation around major failures, cyberattacks and other high-impact incidents.

4. Technology keeps supporting the Nasdaq while the broader market stays mixed

Reuters reported that the Nasdaq reached an intraday record on September 22, helped by major technology stocks. The wider market remained mixed as investors watched energy prices, interest-rate policy and U.S.-China talks. For the AI sector, the picture is continued investor enthusiasm without a uniform rally across the market.

What to watch next

  • the outcome of the UN Security Council discussion on AI,
  • any concrete U.S.-China agreement on AI incident reporting,
  • the first qualified products and deployments under NVIDIA DSX Ready,
  • whether AI-sector momentum persists amid high energy and financing costs.
